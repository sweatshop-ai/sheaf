## What the PR does

- `handler._delete` takes the moves', name's and page's locks through `moves.page_held`: the page's lock only when it is free,
  else the moves' lock is let go, the page waited for alone, and all three taken again. A cancel, a move or a forget no
  longer waits behind a render of a page being deleted.
- `moves.page_held` gains `look=`: run under the moves' and name's lock before the page's lock is tried, again at each round,
  its result yielded. The Delete's owner check (`_own_loose`) runs there, so it never reads through the hub under the
  page's lock, and a page that went while the locks were let go is answered 404. The other callers pass no `look`.
- The early `_own_loose` before any lock stays: it refuses a stranger's or missing page without taking a lock.
- SPEC 9.11 and `lockorder.py`: the Delete exception is gone; Delete joins the move to another tiroir and the restore from
  the trash among the `page_held` users.
- `forwards.drop_leading_to` now runs under the page's lock too (a store lock, innermost; order unchanged).
- Tests: `tests/test_trash.py::test_a_delete_lets_go_of_the_moves_lock_while_it_waits_for_a_render_of_its_page` (red first,
  with the `lock_order` checker) and `test_a_page_that_went_while_a_delete_waited_for_a_render_is_not_found`.
