## What a page edit writes

- [x] **P2: backslashes are lost on a page edit.** *(merged: #475, 2f72e076; probed `a\\b`, `a\*b`, `c\_d` now round-trip, tests/test_edit_backslash.py)*  `html_block_to_md` (`src/sheafmd/edit.py:629`, writer call at `:644`/`:648`) passes paragraph text through pandoc's gfm writer, which leaves `\\` unescaped and swallows the character after an escaped backslash. Probed: typing `a\\b` saves `a\\b` (reads back `a\b`); `a\*b` saves `a\\b` (the `*` is lost); `c\_d` saves `c\\d`. Tables with a break avoid it by going as raw HTML (`edit.py:585-590`); paragraphs do not. Fix idea: escape backslash runs around the pandoc call.
- [x] **Emoji shortcodes become emoji after an edit.** *(merged: #479, 932300d5; tests/test_edit_emoji.py)*  The page renders `:+1:` with gfm's emoji extension as `<span class="emoji" data-emoji="+1">👍</span>`. - [x] **A no-break space counts as white space in `ends_its_cell`** *(merged: #482, cc479e22; tests/test_edit_cell_breaks.py, 2 of its 6 tests red before the last fix; `a<br>&nbsp;` now saves `| a |`, two no-break spaces keep the break and leave one.
