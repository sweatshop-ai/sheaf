# Handoff: ticket 200 done, a first render runs outside the hub's lock (2026-11-26)

**Status:** done. PR #679, merged as 426d9ddf; CI green on 677e1666; review round 8 GO.

## Done

- `Hub._entry` makes a new entry under the hub's lock, then runs its first render (`e.live.refresh(force=True)`) outside that lock. A per-address `_Making` marker lets a second open of the same address wait for the first render; it then receives the same entry, rendered once.
- After the render the maker takes the hub's lock again. If the root was forgotten meanwhile, the address names nothing; if the address was deleted or moved meanwhile, the entry is not kept and is made again from what the address names now.
- Moves (`moved_page`, `moved_remote`) and a forget of the root (`_let_go_root`) detach an entry still being made **before** they touch the versions store. The maker's `forget` then removes only the texts that entry wrote. `moved_remote` detaches without the hub's lock, because its caller holds a file lock; `detach` takes no lock and acts once.
- `_render_held` no longer holds the hub's lock over `_render_entry`. The warnings of a render answered `pending` go back on the entry under the name's and the hub's lock (`hand`), before an open waiting for those locks can read them.
- SPEC 9.11 and `lockorder.py` state that the hub's lock is never held over a pandoc run.
- Tests are in `tests/test_hub_index.py`: boxes A–D, a move away and back during the first render, the late warnings, and a first render ending during a move or a landing on another tiroir (root-local store).
- The flaky late-warnings test (KeyError `warnings` on #688) is fixed: the answer limit is restored before the second open.

## Known limit (accepted, no ticket)

`end_pull` and `_tell_grants` loop over `self.entries` and do not see an entry still in `_making`. A pull can therefore pass `text=None` for a page in its first render, and a grants change can skip it. Both correct themselves at the next pass or open (Codex round 5, minor; the boss decided on 2026-11-26).

## Worth knowing

- `boss_merge` counts a fix-only GO (`--since`) only after a NO-GO or a GO with findings. A rebase that moves the reviewed head off the branch voids `--since` and `--merged-base-since`. Merge main in rather than rebase once a round has a verdict.
