# Raw HTML

A div that spans a blank line:

<div class="notice">
First line inside the div.

Second line after a blank line.
</div>

A details block:

<details>
<summary>More about the bicycle bell</summary>

The bell is rung twice before a junction.

</details>

An image on its own line:

<img src="bell.png" alt="a bell" width="40">

A paragraph with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> and <b>bold</b> inline HTML inside it.

<!-- a single-line comment on its own line -->

A paragraph before a multi-line comment.

<!--
A comment that spans
several lines, with a blank line in it:

and some more text.
-->

A paragraph after the multi-line comment.

<p>A paragraph tag
that closes on the next line.</p>

<br>

The end.
